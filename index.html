About the meetup

TouchPoint is an informal monthly gathering for people who are blind or have low vision, along with family, friends, guide dog handlers, and sighted allies. Come for tea, conversation, and whatever's on people's minds that month — new assistive tech, travel tips, book recommendations, or just catching up.

No agenda, no pressure to speak up if you'd rather just listen. First-timers are always welcome, and you don't need to bring anything but yourself.

What we provide

  • ✓Sighted guides are available at the entrance and throughout the venue — just ask any volunteer wearing a green lanyard.
  • ✓Guide dogs and other service animals are always welcome, with water bowls and a relief area on site.
  • ✓Materials are available in large print, braille, and audio — let us know your preference when you sign up.
  • ✓The room stays low-noise with clear pathways; furniture layout doesn't change between sessions.
  • ✓A quiet room is available if you need a break from the group.
